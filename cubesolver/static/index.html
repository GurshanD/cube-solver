Rubik's Cube Solver

Solves a 2x2 or 3x3 cube using one of four search algorithms: BFS, DFS, iterative deepening DFS, or A*. BFS and DFS only work on short scrambles (around 6 moves on a 3x3). A* can handle about 10.

Building lookup tables for A*, this only happens the first time (about 10 seconds)...
